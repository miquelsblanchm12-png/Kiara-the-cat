# My bot pet

A desktop pet made with Godot 4.7. A pixel art cat walks around on top of your other windows, plays with a ball, and can be dragged with the mouse.

## How it works

The cat and the ball are borderless, transparent, always-on-top windows that move across the usable area of the screen. The cat wanders randomly and sometimes stops to idle. Every so often it runs to the ball and headbutts it, then follows the ball, jumps, and hits it again whenever they touch. The ball has gravity, bounces and rotation, and returns to its corner on its own once it stops.

## Controls

- Left click and drag: move the cat or the ball. The ball is thrown with the speed you release it at.
- F9: send the cat to the bottom right corner and park it. Press again to release it.
- F10: make the cat go to the ball.
